Couples & Relationship Therapy

Space to reconnect — with yourself and each other

Relationship therapy allows you to work through issues in your current relationship, look at how past relationships may be affecting your current one, and explore ways of improving and strengthening your relationships in the future.

I believe my primary role is to help you improve your responses to each other without violating your integrity, core values or deeply held principles.

The major aim of relationship therapy is increasing your knowledge about yourself, your partner and the patterns of interaction between you. Therapy becomes effective as you apply new knowledge to break ineffective patterns and develop better ones.

I draw on the Developmental Model of Couples Therapy, Relational Life Therapy, and other integrative approaches.

Sex Therapy

A space for intimacy, desire and connection

I am trained in Assessing and Treating Sexual Issues in Psychotherapy and am part of an ongoing group to keep up to date with knowledge and training in this field.

Sex therapy is a space to explore challenges or questions around sex, intimacy, desire and connection, without judgement.

People come to sex therapy for all kinds of reasons, including:

  • Feeling disconnected from desire
  • Difficulties with arousal or orgasm
  • Pain or discomfort during sex
  • Navigating different libidos in your relationship
  • The impact of stress, shame, identity or trauma with sexuality
  • Wanting to reconnect with pleasure

Other areas include: Desire discrepancy, vaginismus, vaginal atrophy, delayed/premature ejaculation, erectile dysfunction, and increasing sexual function and pleasure.

LGBTQIA+ Affirming Therapy

Supporting connection, intimacy and authenticity

I offer a warm, affirming space for LGBTQ+ clients to explore who they are. Therapy can be a place to untangle shame, heal from rejection, and gently reconnect with the parts of you that may have been hidden or silenced.

Whether you are exploring your identity, navigating relationships, or simply longing to feel more at home in yourself, you are welcome here.

Couples Therapy Intensive

A concentrated, immersive experience for couples

For couples who want to address relationship issues more quickly than traditional weekly sessions. Available as 3-hour, 1-day, 2-day and 3-day packages, all online. Bookings are by enquiry only.

Why choose an intensive?

  • To develop effective ways of communicating
  • If there has been an infidelity or betrayal requiring immediate support
  • Deciding whether to stay in your relationship or work towards an end
  • Deciding if you would like to open up your relationship
  • Exploring sexual intimacy and creating more emotional depth

How it works

  • All intensives take place online via secure video.
  • Sessions can be consecutive or spread across weeks — we agree a schedule to suit you.
  • The 3-day package includes a 1-hour follow-up session two weeks after the final day.
  • Bookings are by enquiry — get in touch to discuss your needs and availability.

Small Group — Therapy

A structured 12-week psychoeducation group exploring themes of boundaries, consent, relationships and self-understanding. Open to everyone — no clinical referral or previous therapy experience needed.

What the programme covers:

  • Understanding yourself and your patterns
  • Boundaries — what they are, why they matter and how to set them

Programme structure

  • Week 1 — 1.5 hours  ·  Week 2 — 1.5 hours  ·  Week 3 — 1 hour  ·  Week 4 — rest (off)

  • Sessions are not recorded
Please note: Small Group — Therapy is a psychoeducation and personal development group. It is not a substitute for individual therapy or crisis support.

Frequently asked questions

Therapy & sessions

How do I know if therapy is right for me? +
Many people find that talking things through in a safe, non-judgemental space helps them gain perspective, process emotions and make changes. If you are unsure, you are welcome to get in touch and we can have a brief conversation before committing to anything.
Do I need a referral to book? +
No. You can book directly via the booking page. New clients book into released availability — existing regular clients continue to use the current process.
What happens in the first session? +
The first session is an opportunity for us to meet, for you to share a little about what has brought you to therapy, and for us to think together about what you are hoping to get from the work.
What is the cancellation policy? +
48 hours' notice is required to cancel or reschedule a session. Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice and non-attendance are charged at the full session rate.
Do you offer reduced fees? +
A small number of reduced fee spaces are reserved for those experiencing financial difficulty. Please mention this in your enquiry.
What is Small Group — Therapy? +
Small Group — Therapy is a 12-week psychoeducation programme open to everyone — no referral or previous therapy experience needed. It covers boundaries, consent, relationships and self-understanding across 9 sessions. It is not individual therapy. The programme costs £550 per person. Get in touch to find out when the next one starts.

Frequently asked questions

Supervision & membership

What is the difference between Small Group — Supervision and Healing Relationships? +
Small Group — Supervision is small, fixed clinical supervision — a formal professional commitment with a 6-month structure, focused on your client casework. Healing Relationships is a broader learning community — a rolling monthly membership with live sessions, educational resources and peer connection, without a long-term commitment.
Can I join both Small Group — Supervision and Healing Relationships? +
Yes. Many therapists find value in both — Small Group — Supervision for structured clinical supervision of their couples work, and Healing Relationships for broader ongoing learning and community.
Do you supervise trainees? +
Yes. I supervise both qualified therapists and trainees across a range of courses and placements. Please get in touch to discuss your specific training requirements.
What theoretical approach do you use in supervision? +
My supervision draws primarily on Transactional Analysis, Relational and Integrative approaches, and the Developmental Model for Couples Therapy. I am integrative and adapt to the needs of the supervisee and their client work.
Can I join Small Group — Supervision if I am new to couples work? +
Yes. The group welcomes therapists who are new to couples work as well as those with more experience. You should be qualified or in advanced training, hold professional insurance and be a member of a relevant professional body.
Can I cancel my Small Group — Supervision place part-way through? +
No. The full 6-month fee remains payable once you confirm your place, regardless of attendance. Please only sign up if you can commit to the full 6 months.
What is Small Group — Therapy and who is it for? +
Small Group — Therapy is a 12-week psychoeducation group programme open to anyone — you do not need to be a current therapy client. It covers themes including boundaries, consent, relationships and self-understanding. It is a structured group learning experience, not individual therapy. Places are limited (minimum 8, maximum 30). Get in touch to find out when the next programme starts.
Is Small Group — Therapy the same as individual therapy? +
No. Small Group — Therapy is a psychoeducation and personal development group — it is not individual therapy, crisis support or clinical supervision. If you are looking for one-to-one support, please see the individual therapy options.

Frequently Asked
Questions

If you can't find an answer here, please get in touch and I'll be happy to help.

Therapy — general

How do I know if therapy is right for me? +
Many people find that talking things through in a safe, non-judgemental space helps them gain perspective, process emotions and make positive changes. If you are unsure, you are welcome to get in touch — there is no obligation to book after an initial enquiry.
Do I need a referral to book? +
No. You can book directly via the booking page. New clients book into released availability — existing regular clients continue with the current process.
What happens in the first session? +
The first session is an opportunity for us to meet, for you to share a little about what has brought you to therapy, and for us to think together about what you are hoping to get from the work. There is no pressure to share more than you are ready to.
How many sessions will I need? +
This varies considerably depending on what brings you to therapy and what you are hoping to achieve. Some people find a short block of sessions useful; others prefer to work over a longer period. We will review progress together regularly and you are always free to end when you feel ready.
Is everything I say confidential? +
Yes, with the usual professional exceptions. Confidentiality may need to be broken if there is a serious risk of harm to yourself or others, or if required by law. These limits are explained fully in the Terms and Conditions of Service.
Do you offer reduced fees? +
A small number of reduced fee spaces are reserved for those experiencing financial difficulty. These are limited and offered depending on individual circumstances and current availability. Please mention this in your enquiry.

Booking & payments

What is the cancellation policy? +
48 hours' notice is required to cancel or reschedule any individual or couples session. Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice and non-attendance are charged at the full session rate. Group sessions cannot be cancelled or rescheduled by participants.
How do I pay? +
Payment is taken via Stripe at the time of booking for all individual and couples sessions. Receipts are issued automatically. Healing Relationships and Couples Supervision Group payments are also handled via Stripe.
Are sessions recorded? +
No. Sessions are never recorded. You will be asked to confirm this at booking.
What platform are sessions held on? +
Sessions are held via Zoom. A unique, secure link is sent with your booking confirmation. You must attend from a private space where you will not be interrupted or overheard.
Do you hold a policy with WPA Insurance? +
I am registered with WPA Insurance. If you hold a WPA policy, please send an enquiry before booking directly and mention your policy — this means we can arrange things correctly for you from the start. Please do not book a session directly without contacting me first if you intend to use your WPA cover.

Couples therapy & intensive

One of us isn't sure about coming — is that okay? +
Yes. Ambivalence is very common when entering couples therapy. An initial session can be a helpful way to explore whether therapy is right for you at this time, without any commitment to ongoing work.
What is a couples intensive? +
A couples intensive is a concentrated block of therapy — from 3 hours up to 3 days — allowing deeper work in a shorter timeframe than weekly sessions. It is available online and bookings are by enquiry.
Can we do a couples intensive and then continue with weekly sessions? +
Yes. Some couples use an intensive as a starting point and then continue with regular sessions. Others find the intensive stands alone. We discuss what would be most useful for you at the enquiry stage.

Clinical supervision

Do you supervise trainees? +
Yes. I supervise both qualified therapists and trainees across a range of courses and placements. Please get in touch to discuss your specific training requirements.
Can I join Small Group — Supervision if I am new to couples work? +
Yes. The groups welcome therapists who are new to couples work as well as those with more experience. You should be qualified or in advanced training, hold professional insurance and be a member of a relevant professional body.
What happens if Small Group — Supervision doesn't reach the minimum number? +
A group requires a minimum of 4 participants. If the minimum is not reached, you will be offered a place in the next available group or a full refund.

Group membership

Are Healing Relationships live sessions recorded? +
No. Live sessions are never recorded. This protects the confidentiality of all members. The pre-recorded educational content is separate and available at any time.
Can I access content after I cancel my Couples Therapy Collective membership? +
No. Access to all member-only content ends at the end of your final paid month.
